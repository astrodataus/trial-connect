Scenario

Shortlisting a trial

Who you are

An oncologist or care navigator in a value-based oncology network, in clinic, with a patient in front of you.

What you are here to do

Shortlist recruiting clinical trials this patient might be eligible for, and see exactly why each one surfaced. The app never asserts eligibility; you judge the criteria, it does the searching.

Your goals

  • Check a patient’s attributes against every recruiting study, in seconds instead of an hour of protocol reading.
  • Read the eligibility criteria verbatim, with the matched sentences shown, and see near-misses a phone call could resolve.
  • Weigh travel: the nearest recruiting site, and what sits within the patient’s stated radius.

The data

A ClinicalTrials.gov snapshot from 22 Aug 2026: 5,980 recruiting studies and 37,593 US sites across breast, NSCLC, colorectal and prostate cancer. Eight synthetic patient profiles; no real patient data anywhere.

What you can find

  • The Gap · how many recruiting studies are within reach of the demo panel, and where options run thin.
  • Match · a ranked shortlist per patient, with hard filters and soft signals kept visibly distinct.
  • The Study · one trial in full: verbatim criteria, every US site, contacts, this patient’s match breakdown.
  • The Map · recruiting sites against the patient’s location, with distance rings at 25, 50 and 100 miles.
  • The Landscape · recruiting studies by tumor type, phase and sponsor class.
